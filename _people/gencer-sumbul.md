Dr. Gencer Sumbul is a tenure-track faculty member at [INSAIT](https://insait.ai/), where he is building a research group on AI for Earth observation (EO) and environmental intelligence. His research lies at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and remote sensing, with a current focus on multimodal geospatial foundation models and their use in environmental applications, including deforestation mapping, wildlife monitoring, species distribution modeling, and climate extremes.

He joins INSAIT from EPFL where he was a postdoctoral scientist at the Environmental Computational Science and Earth Observation ([ECEO](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/eceo/)) Laboratory led by [Prof. Devis Tuia](https://people.epfl.ch/devis.tuia). He earned his PhD *summa cum laude* in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from TU Berlin under the supervision of [Prof. Begüm Demir](https://www.bifold.berlin/people/prof-dr-beguem-demir.html). Prior to this, he received his MSc and BSc in Computer Engineering from Bilkent University.

Dr. Gencer Sumbul is the creator of [BigEarthNet](https://bigearth.net/), one of the landmark data foundations for modern EO research, and of [SMARTIES](https://gsumbul.github.io/SMARTIES/), a geospatial foundation model that unifies large-scale knowledge discovery across diverse remote sensing sensors. He has contributed to several European Space Agency and Horizon Europe research projects as co-principal investigator and key researcher, aiming at scalable and generalizable AI for environmental and societal understanding through EO.
